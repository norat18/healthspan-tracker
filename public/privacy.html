Privacy & Terms

Last updated November 11, 2026

In short: your health data belongs to you. Without an account it never leaves your browser. With an account it’s end-to-end encrypted on your device before it’s saved, so only you can read it — not the app’s developer, not Google, not Cloudflare. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and you can delete everything at any time.

What Healthspan Tracker stores

  • What you enter: test results, scan and visit notes, screening and vaccine dates, supplements and medications, lifestyle changes, family history, home measurements, your date of birth and sex.
  • If you sign in with Google: your Google account’s name, email address and profile picture (used only to show who is signed in), and a user ID from Firebase.

We do not collect your location, contacts, device identifiers or browsing activity. There are no cookies for advertising or analytics.

Where your data lives

  • Not signed in: only in your web browser’s storage on that device. Clearing your browser data deletes it.
  • Signed in: in a single private record in Google Cloud Firestore (United States), plus a copy in your browser for offline use, and up to 30 daily copies for “Restore an earlier version”.
  • End-to-end encrypted. Before anything is saved, your device compresses it and encrypts it with AES-256-GCM using a random key that is created on your device and never sent anywhere. The database stores only that unreadable ciphertext (plus the time it was saved). Security rules refuse to save anything that isn’t encrypted.
  • Your recovery key is shown to you once when you set up your account (and any time in the Data tab). You type it in to open your data on a new device or after signing out. Signing out removes the key from that device. If you lose your recovery key, your saved data can’t be recovered by anyone — you can start over with an empty account.
  • Security rules also allow only your signed-in account to read or change your record, and Firebase App Check helps block requests that don’t come from this app.

Importing reports

When you import a PDF or a photo of a report, it is read on your device. The file is never uploaded. For scanned reports and photos, the app downloads a text-recognition engine (Tesseract, from the jsDelivr CDN) to do this in your browser; no report content is sent with that download. Only the values you choose to save are stored.

Who can see it

Only you. Healthspan Tracker does not sell, rent, share or use your data for advertising, research or AI training. The service providers that run the app — Google (Firebase hosting, sign-in and database) and Cloudflare (website hosting) — process data only to provide those services. Because your data is end-to-end encrypted, the app’s maintainer can’t read it either — not even with full access to the database — and couldn’t hand it over if asked.

What the maintainer can see: that an account exists and its Google name, email and profile picture (from sign-in); when it last saved and roughly how much data it has; and, if you turn on reminders, the dates something is due (not what it is).

The one thing encryption can’t protect against is a change to the app’s own code, since that code runs on your device. The app’s code is public on GitHub and is published only by an automated build from that repository, so any change is visible.

Your choices

  • Download a copy of your data any time (Data tab → Download a copy).
  • Delete everything: Data tab → Delete my account and data removes your record and your sign-in account permanently.
  • Reminder notifications are off unless you turn them on. When on, only due dates (for example “something is due Dec 7, 2026” — not what it is) and your device’s notification address are stored by our reminder service on Cloudflare, so it knows when to notify you. The notification itself carries no content; your device fills in the text. No results, names of tests or other health details are sent. Turning notifications off, or deleting your account, removes this.

Connections (optional)

  • Strava: if you connect it, your browser fetches your recent activities directly from Strava and keeps only weekly totals (cardio minutes and strength sessions), encrypted with the rest of your data. Individual workouts, routes and locations aren’t saved. Our reminder service is used only to complete the Strava sign-in (it holds the app’s Strava credentials); it stores nothing and never sees your activities. The Strava sign-in tokens are kept inside your encrypted data. You can disconnect any time in Data → Connections. Strava data is never used for AI or shared.
  • Food logs (MyFitnessPal, Cronometer and similar CSV exports) are read on your device; only weekly counts of days meeting each habit are saved.

If something goes wrong

If there is ever a breach of security that exposes your data, we will notify affected users and the U.S. Federal Trade Commission as required by the FTC Health Breach Notification Rule.

Terms of use

  • Not medical advice. Healthspan Tracker is a personal record-keeping tool. Targets, screening schedules and scores (such as fitness age) are general information from published guidelines and may not fit you. Always talk to a qualified clinician about your results and care.
  • No emergency use. Don’t rely on the app for urgent or emergency health decisions.
  • Provided “as is”. The app is free and open source (MIT License). We do our best to keep it working and your data safe, but can’t guarantee it will always be available or error-free. Keep your own copy of anything important.
  • Your responsibility: keep your Google account secure, and only enter data you have the right to store.
  • We may update these terms; the date above shows the latest version.
